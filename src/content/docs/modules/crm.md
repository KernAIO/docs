A light but complete CRM for teams that sell as well as build.

- **Contacts** and **companies** with custom fields, tags and ownership.
- **Leads** and **deals** with **pipelines** (kanban; stage changes use the workflow engine, so approvals and automations apply).
- **Activities**: notes, tasks, calls (via Calls), emails (linked from the Mail inbox) on a timeline per record.
- **Web-to-lead** forms, CSV imports, duplicate detection.
- Reports: pipeline value by stage, forecast-lite, won/lost by period.
- Deals and contacts are objects like any other: mention them in chat, embed in docs, attach files, open an object channel.
